Nihongo Quiz Replies: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This app is used only by the Facebook Page 日語教室Podcast to reply to comments on its own quiz posts.

What it reads

Comments that people post publicly on the Page's quiz posts: the comment's text, its ID and its time.

What it does

It posts a reply to the comment from the Page, and likes it.

What it keeps

The comment's ID, the reply that was posted and the time. It does not keep commenters' names. A working copy of the comment text is held on the operator's computer and overwritten on each run.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone, and nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Deletion

Delete your comment on Facebook, or send a message to the Page and any record of it will be removed.

Contact

Send a message to the Page: facebook.com/nihongokyoshitsutw

Nihongo Quiz Replies：隱私權政策

本應用程式僅供 Facebook 粉絲專頁「日語教室Podcast」使用，用來回覆專頁測驗貼文下的留言。

讀取的資料

大家在專頁測驗貼文下公開發表的留言：留言內容、留言編號與時間。

用途

以專頁的身分回覆該則留言，並對留言按讚。

保存的資料

留言編號、已發出的回覆內容與時間。不保存留言者的姓名。留言內容只會暫存在操作者的電腦上，每次執行時都會被覆蓋。

分享

不會與任何人分享或販售資料，也不會用於廣告或個人分析。

刪除

您可以在 Facebook 上刪除自己的留言，或傳訊息給專頁，我們會移除相關紀錄。

聯絡方式

請傳訊息給專頁：facebook.com/nihongokyoshitsutw
